About Insurance Help

Transforming what insured and insurer both need into a fair result

Insurance Help was built on a simple idea: policyholders deserve a team in their corner who understands construction, reads policy language for a living, and knows how carriers negotiate.

Our mission

Innovative solutions for today's dynamic claims environment

At Insurance Help, our team of experts is committed to providing innovative, honest solutions that meet the real demands of a claim — not the simplified version an initial estimate suggests. Our goal is to empower policyholders with the same technical footing insurance companies bring to the table, so the outcome reflects the actual loss.

That means combining hands-on building knowledge with claims and appraisal experience under one roof, rather than sending you between separate specialists who don't talk to each other.

Credentials

Licensed to represent you, not the carrier

Public adjusters are state-licensed professionals, held to the same regulatory standard as the adjusters insurance companies employ — the difference is who we work for. Our team of experts maintains active licensing across the states we serve, so you're always represented by someone qualified to go toe-to-toe with the carrier.

  • Licensed public adjusters, bonded per state requirements
  • Contracts and disclosures filed per state insurance department rules
  • Building consulting background informs every estimate
Beyond licensing

Specialized training our team brings to every claim

On top of state adjuster licensing, our team holds industry-recognized designations in restoration, remediation, and building diagnostics — so complex losses get evaluated with the right expertise, not guesswork.

Master Water Restorer

Advanced-level training in water damage restoration, drying science, and loss mitigation.

Applied Microbial Remediation Specialist

Certified in identifying and remediating mold and microbial contamination after water and storm losses.

HAAG Certified

Trained under the HAAG Engineering standard for evaluating hail, wind, and roofing damage.

Professional Building Analyst

Skilled in diagnosing structural and building-envelope issues to support accurate, defensible estimates.
